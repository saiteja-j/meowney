Meowney privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Meowney is a small personal money tracker that runs in your phone's browser. This page explains what happens to your data.

Your entries stay on your device

Everything you enter (expenses, income, categories, budgets, settings and bill photos) is stored in your browser's storage on your own device. The developer has no server and cannot see your data.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on Google Drive backup, Meowney asks Google for one permission: to store its own backup file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). Meowney cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

  • The backup file contains the same information as the app's backup file: your entries, categories, budgets, settings and bill photos.
  • The file is sent directly from your device to your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any other service.
  • When you sign in, the app loads Google's sign-in script from Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
  • Your Google email address may be shown inside the app so you can see which account is connected. It is kept only on your device.

Google API data policy

Meowney's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning it off and deleting your data

  • In Meowney, open Settings, then Google Drive backup, then Turn off. This keeps the copy already in your Drive.
  • To remove Meowney's access completely, open your Google Account, go to third-party access, and remove Meowney. You can also delete its hidden data there.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, erases the entries stored on your device.

No ads, no tracking

Meowney has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
